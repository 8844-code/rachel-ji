One more storyteller.

多一个讲述者。

"One less storyteller means one less piece of history for humanity." — Wings of Desire

I grew up in an ethnic minority region of southwest China and graduated in Digital Media Arts from Southwest Forestry University.

我成长于中国西南少数民族地区，毕业于西南林业大学数字媒体艺术专业。

My learning and creative practice span audiovisual language, screenwriting, film sound, photography, and camera blocking. I have also worked across television news, documentary production, museum content, retail content, and digital media.

我的学习与创作涉及视听语言、剧本创作、影视声音、摄影与镜头调度，同时也做过电视新闻、纪录片、博物馆内容、门店内容和数字媒体工作。

My experience as a creator showed me how unclear the path can be between finishing a script, getting it discovered, and receiving licensing interest. That led me to build and launch ScriptBridge—a prototype for presenting Chinese-language scripts and receiving licensing requests, designed to make the connection between creators and potential users clearer.

创作者的经历让我看见，一部剧本从完成创作到被看见、提出授权申请，中间仍有许多模糊环节。因此，我搭建并上线了 ScriptBridge：一个面向中文剧本展示与授权申请的产品原型，希望把创作者与潜在使用者之间的路径变得更清楚。

Alongside online English teaching, I am developing an AI-assisted learning and action system. It breaks goals into manageable steps and creates a loop of real action, visible results, emotional feedback, and the next plan. The aim is to help people with ADHD—and others who struggle to start or finish tasks—move toward learning and action goals with less pressure.

现在，我在从事线上英语教学的同时，也在构建一套 AI 协作学习与行动系统：把目标拆成可执行的步骤，通过真实行动、可见成果、感受反馈与下一步安排形成闭环，希望帮助 ADHD 人群以及其他难以启动或完成任务的人，以更低压力的方式推进学习与行动目标。

How I work.

我的工作。

I have a background in Digital Media Arts and currently work as an online English teacher. I turn recurring problems in teaching, creative work, and learning into practical systems. I define the problem, structure the workflow, evaluate the output, and use AI to accelerate implementation and iteration.

The proof is not a tool list. It is what runs, what gets used, and what I learn from it.

我有数字媒体艺术背景，现在是一名线上英语老师。我把教学、创作和学习中反复出现的问题，整理成能够实际运行的系统。我负责定义问题、组织流程、判断结果，再让 AI 帮我更快地实现和迭代。

证据不是工具清单，而是什么真正跑起来、被使用，并让我继续改进。

AI-assisted Learning & Action System
AI 协作学习与行动系统
Who it helps
帮助谁
Helps people with ADHD—and others who struggle to organize, start, or finish tasks—move toward their goals with less pressure.
帮助 ADHD 人群以及其他难以安排、启动或完成任务的人，以更低压力的方式推进目标。
What it does
解决什么
Turns a goal into manageable actions, makes progress visible, and adjusts the pace through both completion evidence and emotional feedback.
把目标变成可以完成的行动，让进展看得见，并同时依据完成证据与真实感受调整节奏。
How it works
怎样运行
01 Understand you了解你
Capture goals, personal patterns, current capacity, voice notes, and screenshots.
接收目标、个人特点、当下状态，以及语音、截图等输入。
02 Set the rhythm安排节奏
AI organizes the input and sets an adjustable 7-day, 10-day, or one-month action cycle based on the goal, available time, and personal capacity. Longer cycles are avoided to keep feedback timely.
AI 整理与分流信息，根据目标、可用时间和个人状态，生成可调整的 7 天、10 天或 1 个月行动周期；避免周期过长，让反馈保持及时。
03 Take one action低压行动
Give one manageable next action with a limited finish line instead of a growing task list.
一次只给出一个有明确终点的动作，不让任务不断扩大。
04 Record facts & feelings记录事实与感受
Keep what was done, visible evidence, and how the process actually felt.
不只记录做了什么，也记录成果证据和完成时的真实感受。
05 Review & continue回顾并继续
AI recognizes progress, adjusts the pace from real feedback, and generates the next step.
AI 承认已经发生的完成，根据真实反馈调整节奏，再生成下一步。
This is not only a tracker. The system is designed to understand both progress and the human experience behind it, helping users build a warmer and more sustainable sense of momentum.
它不只是一个打卡工具，而是同时理解进展与人的感受，帮助用户建立更有温度、也更可持续的行动感。

Excerpt — Chapter One
节选 — 第一章

电梯门徐徐打开，家门展现在眼前，随之而来的，还有门内玻璃破碎的声音，刺耳争吵的声音。

他抬起手指按在指纹识别键上——

"指纹识别错误"
"指纹识别错误"
"指纹识别错误"

终于在第四次，清脆的提示音响起，门开了。

一地狼藉。激烈的争吵立刻灌入耳膜，碎玻璃渣反射着天花板上吊灯的光——一地五彩的闪亮。

The elevator doors opened slowly. The front door came into view — and with it, the sound of breaking glass, the sound of screaming.

He pressed his finger to the lock —

Fingerprint not recognized.
Fingerprint not recognized.
Fingerprint not recognized.

On the fourth try, a clear chime. The door opened.

Everything in pieces. The argument flooded his ears. Shards of glass caught the light from the chandelier above — scattered across the floor like something almost beautiful.

宋叶星 (Sông Yèxīng) begins high school the autumn after his parents' violent divorce. A story about fracture and beginning — the summer that disappeared from memory, and the year he tried to start over.
宋叶星在父母激烈离婚的那个暑假之后升入高中。一个关于破碎与重新开始的故事——那个消失在记忆里的暑假，和他试图重新开始的那一年。
