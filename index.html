About
关于我

One more storyteller.

多一个讲述者。

"One less storyteller means one less piece of history for humanity." — Wings of Desire
"少一个讲述者，人类就少一段历史。" ——《柏林苍穹下》
"I want to tell people things they don't know, show them what they can't see." — Yang Yang, Yi Yi (2000)
"我要去告诉别人他们不知道的事，给别人看他们看不到的东西。" ——洋洋，《一一》

I came to filmmaking and asked: where do I start? Then I watched Wings of Desire and understood — the camera and I had found each other. I picked it up with impulsive conviction and tried to find philosophy through images, find the world.

我接触影视的时候问自己：该从哪里开始呢？直到看了《柏林苍穹下》才明白——摄像机和我是找到彼此了。我带着冲动的偏执抬起它，试图用影像来发现哲学、来发现世界。

Raised in an ethnic minority region of southwest China. Studied Digital Media Arts at Southwest Forestry University. My research: audiovisual language, film sound, cinematography, camera blocking. I've worked in TV news, documentary production, and film festival coverage. Now I teach English, build platforms with AI, and keep writing the novel that's been with me for years.

成长于中国西南少数民族地区。就读于西南林业大学数字媒体艺术专业。研究方向：视听语言剧本创作、影视声音艺术、摄影艺术、镜头调度。做过电视台外采、纪录片、影展摄像。现在教英语，用AI构建平台，也继续写那部写了很多年的小说。

Excerpt — Chapter One
节选 — 第一章

电梯门徐徐打开，家门展现在眼前，随之而来的，还有门内玻璃破碎的声音，刺耳争吵的声音。

他抬起手指按在指纹识别键上——

"指纹识别错误"
"指纹识别错误"
"指纹识别错误"

终于在第四次，清脆的提示音响起，门开了。

一地狼藉。激烈的争吵立刻灌入耳膜，碎玻璃渣反射着天花板上吊灯的光——一地五彩的闪亮。

The elevator doors opened slowly. The front door came into view — and with it, the sound of breaking glass, the sound of screaming.

He pressed his finger to the lock —

Fingerprint not recognized.
Fingerprint not recognized.
Fingerprint not recognized.

On the fourth try, a clear chime. The door opened.

Everything in pieces. The argument flooded his ears. Shards of glass caught the light from the chandelier above — scattered across the floor like something almost beautiful.

宋叶星 (Sông Yèxīng) begins high school the autumn after his parents' violent divorce. A story about fracture and beginning — the summer that disappeared from memory, and the year he tried to start over.
宋叶星在父母激烈离婚的那个暑假之后升入高中。一个关于破碎与重新开始的故事——那个消失在记忆里的暑假，和他试图重新开始的那一年。
